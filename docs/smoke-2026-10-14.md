**Known limits**
- Devanagari text measures 10–25% wider than it draws (the measuring library does not join letter clusters), so Nepali and Hindi lines wrap a little early but never overflow. A text shaper would fix it later.
- The blog page's own headings ("Key takeaways", "Published") stay English.
- Comics are English only.
- A Nepali post's prices in rupees still come through the market version (open question).
