# ADR-090 · Dependants are asked about properly; parallel lanes are grouped by facet

## Decision

- When the student says they have dependants (opening screen 1 or the composer), a short follow-up asks: partner (name optional, wants to work?, field or occupation, English score if known) and each child (name optional, age, schooling needs). This is where Anita and Aarav come from; until answered, their lanes show "Tell us about Anita to see her road".
- Parallel lanes (Suman's board) are grouped by **facet** — Study, Work and income, Location and commute, Schooling and care, Visa and PR — with each facet visually separated, so a change shows which part of whose life it moves.

## Reasoning

The lanes showed facts about Anita and Aarav with no visible place they were captured (Nikhil, 2 Oct); and mixing study, work and location in one row hides what a choice actually changes.
