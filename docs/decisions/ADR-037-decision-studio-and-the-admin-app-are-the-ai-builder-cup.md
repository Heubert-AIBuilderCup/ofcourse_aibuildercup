# ADR-037 · Decision Studio and the admin app are the AI Builder Cup entry

## Decision

The Decision Studio and the admin app are both part of the Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026 entry.

## Reasoning

- Sets the priority: the parts of this plan that make a working demo of both come first; CRM integration and Stage-2 gates come after.

## Constrains

Deadline two weeks from 30 Sep 2026; a working prototype is enough, and not every path needs to be covered. Scope the entry to: Ask panel intake, board with one route and gates, weights plaque, Compare view, route comics from the type library, admin app with blueprint → approve → render and the frequency-ranked gap list.
