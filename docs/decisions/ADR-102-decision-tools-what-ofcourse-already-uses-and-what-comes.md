# ADR-102 · Decision tools: what OfCourse already uses, and what comes later

## Decision

OfCourse is described as built from established decision tools: decision tree (the board), scenario planning (three futures), weighted matrix (weights and good-and-bad), values clarification (coins), second-order thinking (flexibility), a light pre-mortem (gates with ways past), reversible decisions (keep options open), satisficing (fold rules), and asking before deciding. Future additions, not in the hackathon: a **mind map** of what is on the student's mind that the composer turns into facts, tags and goals; 10/10/10 reflections; a full pre-mortem; regret minimisation; pairwise comparison for ranking; scenario stress tests; a one-route SWOT; a decision journal; an Eisenhower view of tasks; a coaching-style "talk it through" mode; and **value reinforcement**: after she locks in a route, revisiting it first asks "what has changed?"; with no new facts it reminds her of her own reasons and how to live with or offset the trade-offs she accepted; with new facts it reopens the decision. Switching is always allowed, never the default answer to second thoughts. Detail in `README-documents.md`.

## Reasoning

Naming the tools makes the product's logic legible to judges and partners, and gives a roadmap grounded in methods people already trust (Nikhil, 3 Oct).
