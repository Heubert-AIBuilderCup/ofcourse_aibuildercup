# ADR-104 · Role Worlds: step into a job role, adapted from Conceptual PlayWorld for ages 15 and up

## Decision

The evolution of tasters (ADR-076): a 10–15 minute **Role World** per field, structured like a Conceptual PlayWorld: (1) a story entry (a realistic first-week scenario); (2) the student takes the role, with the duck as a colleague and a character who needs help; (3) a realistic problem solved with simplified versions of the role's real tools; (4) emotional imagination: helping a person, not passing a quiz; (5) a debrief: what people in the role do daily, skills used, courses that lead there, sourced demand and pay; then "How did that feel?" as a self-reported fit signal. Never scored as a test; never used for eligibility or ranking.

## Reasoning

Conceptual PlayWorlds use story, roles and emotional imagination to make technical problem-solving approachable for young children (referenced by Nikhil from the Victorian Government's co-teaching model, 3 Oct); the same structure can let older students feel what a job is like before choosing a course toward it.

## Constrains

**Not in the hackathon.** Future: Role Worlds authored with practitioners per field; any facts in the debrief bound to sources; Gen AI may draft scenarios from occupation data, reviewed before use. Admin side: a **Case studio** (research → draft → practitioner review → fact and safety review → playtest → publish), **campaigns** that release cases (with optional events and labelled promotion) to matched audiences, an **opportunity editor** for jobs and internships, case and campaign insights feeding the ideas queue, and review-by dates. Execution detail in `FUTURE-additions.md` Parts 2 and 2b.
