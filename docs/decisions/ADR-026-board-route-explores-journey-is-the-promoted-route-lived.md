# ADR-026 · Board route explores; Journey is the promoted route lived against

## Decision

On the board, a route walk is for exploring: any course, its steps and gates. The Journey tab shows only the promoted route, with real dates, documents and progress the student lives against.

## Reasoning

- They were the same thing drawn twice. Exploring and committing are different acts and deserve different surfaces.

## Constrains

Promotion (ADR-011) is the handover from board to Journey. Journey reads the plan record; the board reads the engine.
