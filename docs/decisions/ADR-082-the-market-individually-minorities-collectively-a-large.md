# ADR-082 · The market: individually minorities, collectively a large underserved group; the course decision is the wedge, the whole new life is the territory

## Decision

- The product's market framing: immigrants and international students are a minority in every place they land, but as a group they are large and chronically underserved, facing many decisions in a new culture with the information asymmetry against them: where to live, what to buy, how to travel, how to bank, how to stay within the rules.
- The **course and visa decision is the wedge** (the Decision Studio); the **companion** (ADR-050) extends the same guide into the first years of the new life, one decision at a time, each as information with sources, matched to the student's route and goals.
- Sequencing holds (ADR-050): content-led guidance for those decisions can start early; paid services around them later.

## Reasoning

This is the honest size of the problem and the clearest statement of who the product is for; the wedge keeps it buildable (Nikhil, 2 Oct). The guard is to stay tied to the student's own route and goals rather than becoming a generic expat app.
