# ADR-095 · The product is called OfCourse

## Decision

The product is **OfCourse**: the student app is **OfCourse**, the marketing and review app is **OfCourse Admin**. The **Decision Studio** stays the name of the exploration surface inside OfCourse (the board, futures and routes). Feed author and bylines read "OfCourse"; Instagram page brands keep neutral names (Main, Cerebral, Infographics, Comics). No internal product names appear anywhere in the hackathon entry.

## Reasoning

One public name for the entry, distinct from internal systems (Nikhil, 2 Oct); OfCourse was already the name of the student app the Decision Studio grew out of.

## Constrains

Repo, deployed URLs, README, video and write-up use "OfCourse". Check the name for conflicts (trademark, domain, app stores) before any public launch beyond the hackathon.
