# ADR-066 · Routes continue past the course: jobs, income during study, money basics

## Decision

Every route carries on after graduation into **life after**: job prospects for the field in that city (demand, salary bands, who hires), **income during study** (part-time work within visa rules, internships, paid placements), and **money basics** (costs, saving, what a student visa allows). Shown as a segment of the route detail and as learning content on the road (ADR-050).

## Rules

- Information with sources, never financial or career advice; investment content in Australia is regulated, so wording is reviewed (ADR-018 tier).
- Salary and demand figures carry the same data states as everything else (estimated, out of date, unknown).

## Reasoning

Students are choosing a life, not a course; the years after the course are most of it (Nikhil, 1 Oct).

## Constrains

Route object gains an after-course segment fed by the catalog's salary, occupation and work-rights data. Hackathon: one static "Life after" strip on Priya's ECU route.
