# ADR-027 · An account is required before the Decision Studio

## Decision

Students sign up before entering the Studio. There is no anonymous exploration.

## Reasoning

- Every answer lands on a record from the first question, which the storage decision (ADR-013) assumes.
- Sign-up is also where the persona question is asked (ADR-029).

## Constrains

Ask and the Studio sit behind login. The public site remains the Unaware-stage surface.
