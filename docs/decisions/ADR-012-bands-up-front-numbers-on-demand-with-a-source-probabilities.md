# ADR-012 · Bands up front; numbers on demand with a source; probabilities never shown to students; staff see everything

## Decision

Up front (board, comics, Compare) trade-offs read as bands and plain words. On demand, on every card and in the student's profile, every cost and time figure is available with its source and check date. PR likelihood is shown to students only as a band, never a percentage; what drives the band (occupation list, regional points, age, English) may be shown. Risk stays as gates with rule and source on tap. Admin and counsellor views show everything, including the internal PR estimate and its inputs.

## Reasoning

- Numbers up front invite false precision; numbers on demand serve the student who wants to think hard (Nikhil).
- A PR percentage claims precision the data lacks; the catalog marks it internal for that reason.
- Staff need the full picture to understand the client (Nikhil).

## Constrains

Every quoted figure carries source and date. The student-facing quotable rule applies to the portal only. Resolves Q3 / D3.
