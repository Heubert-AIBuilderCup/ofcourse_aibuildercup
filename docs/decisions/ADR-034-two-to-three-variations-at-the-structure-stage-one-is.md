# ADR-034 · Two to three variations at the structure stage; one is rendered

## Decision

The AI offers two to three design variations at the structure stage. The editor picks one, and only that one is rendered.

## Reasoning

- Variation is cheap before the render and expensive after (ADR-007).

## Constrains

Art-only re-renders after the fact remain available (A2.5).
