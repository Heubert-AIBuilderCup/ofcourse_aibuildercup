# ADR-043 · Design direction: Walk (1c) — the duck leads

## Decision

Of the three v5 directions, **Walk** is chosen: map-first and warm, the board as the hero with the node card beneath, the duck as the guide that hops between tiles and voices Ask in first person, compare cards dealt over the board. Desktop follows the same direction.

## Reasoning

- It carries the delight of v4 into the new model and reads well in a two-minute demo (Nikhil).
- Its route detail already lands the rules: the verdict with a why, bands with numbers on tap, gates with ways past, documents per stage, the comic with a captioned voice.

## Constrains

Fixes required in the next round: use the empty board space as undiscovered map that fills in (fog of war); anchor the weights plaque to Point A; one band treatment (bars + words) on detail and Compare alike; sequence the confirmation chip before the next question; remove "compare countries" from the preference row (comparison is always on); replace "biggest fork" with "changes your board the most"; poster frames on every video; the duck may be warm but facts come from a source line, not from the duck. States still to review: the re-rank highlight, a sealed course opened from the board, a tapped band. Supersedes the "three directions" request; Atlas and Console are retired (their assumptions stay useful as notes).
