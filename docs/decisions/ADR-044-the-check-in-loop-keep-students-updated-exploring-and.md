# ADR-044 · The check-in loop: keep students updated, exploring and learning — without streaks

## Decision

Students are brought back over time by three mechanisms, none of them streaks, points or badges:

1. **"Something moved on your board."** When a rule, fee, intake or course on a ranked route changes, or a relevant scholarship or opportunity appears, one message with a tap that opens the board with the change highlighted (built on ADR-039).
2. **Event-driven profile check-ins.** After a test date: "Did you sit IELTS? Update your score." After an intake passes: "Still aiming for February?" Every \~90 days on the answers that drive the most (budget, settle abroad): "Still true?" with a one-tap "same". One question, in Ask, in the guide's voice. Never on a schedule the student didn't cause.
3. **The road ahead.** Learning posts tied to the stages of the student's ranked routes (how the visa works, the Genuine Student statement, work rules, real rents), surfaced in the feed as each stage approaches; reading one marks progress on the road so the map fills in as they learn.

## Reasoning

- Streaks would cheapen a life decision; a board that visibly changes because the world changed is a reason to come back that is true (Nikhil, 30 Sep).
- Fresh data is what makes impact updates and the counsellor briefing worth anything; check-ins keep it fresh without a form.
- Stage-tied learning turns the feed from entertainment into progression.

## Constrains

Everything is labelled with why. Opportunity messages are capped weekly; impact messages are not. One switch quiets all of it. Check-ins never rewrite a field; they propose, the student confirms (ADR-010). Adds to F1/F3 in the System Requirements and to the Ask panel.

## Feed layers (added 30 Sep)

The check-in loop sits on top of the general feed; it does not replace it (ADR-016). The feed has three layers: **general posts** (the bulk; everyone), **featured** (especially high-quality posts shown to everyone, prominently, for a limited time; chosen by an editor or earned by performance measured on saves, shares and completion, not views), and **personal** (impact updates, check-ins, opportunities, stage-tied learning; always labelled). Starting mix: at most one personal item in every three or four posts; featured posts pinned for a limited time. Tune from the metrics (S8).
