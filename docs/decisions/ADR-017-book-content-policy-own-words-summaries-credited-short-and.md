# ADR-017 · Book content policy: own-words summaries, credited, short and rare quotes, human-checked

## Decision

Book posts summarise a book's ideas in our own words, credit the author and title with a link, keep quotes short and rare, use no cover images without permission, and are checked by a human before publishing.

## Reasoning

- Copyright protects wording, not ideas; a credited summary is what reviews and reading lists do.
- Long or frequent quotes, or an author's framework reproduced step by step, drift from idea to expression.
- Cover art is the publisher's.

## Constrains

Applies to the dedicated book page and to book posts anywhere. Not legal advice: get a lawyer's view if the page becomes a major channel. Resolves D10.
