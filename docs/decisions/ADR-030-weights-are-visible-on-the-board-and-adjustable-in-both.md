# ADR-030 · Weights are visible on the board and adjustable in both places

## Decision

A small weights plaque sits next to Point A on the board; tapping it opens the sliders. The full sliders also live in the Ask panel.

## Reasoning

- The student should see how the ranking is being decided from where they are standing (ADR-009), and be able to change it without leaving the board.

## Constrains

Both controls edit the same weights on the student record.
