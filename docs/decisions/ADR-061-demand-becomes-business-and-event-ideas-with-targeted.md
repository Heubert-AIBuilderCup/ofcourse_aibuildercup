# ADR-061 · Demand becomes business and event ideas, with targeted audiences

## Decision

The admin ideas queue gains **expansion ideas**: workshops, events and services suggested from what students seek (e.g. a financial-counselling workshop from money questions on the Perth route; a mental-health workshop from month-two anxiety questions; an anger-management class from questions about conflict with housemates or at work; an investment-basics event). Each shows its evidence (how many students, which routes and phases), a suggested format, and its target audience from the audience matcher. Events are an update type (ADR-039) that reaches exactly those students.

## Reasoning

The product already sees demand the business cannot; turning it into targeted events and services is the most direct marketing there is (Nikhil, 1 Oct).

## Constrains

Mental-health content and workshops are support and signposting, never a substitute for care, with a clear path to professional help. Requires a user base; sequenced with the companion (ADR-050).
