# ADR-031 · Fully separate brand guidelines per Instagram page

## Decision

Each Instagram page (comics, cerebral posts, infographics, book summaries, and any future page) has its own brand guidelines: logo, colours, fonts and tone of voice. There is no shared master brand imposed across pages.

## Reasoning

- The pages target different audiences and should feel like different voices (Nikhil).

## Constrains

A brand is a first-class record in the admin app; every template, structure and render is tied to one brand. The portal feed is its own brand.
