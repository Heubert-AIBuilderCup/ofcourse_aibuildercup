# ADR-050 · The companion: Heubert stays with the student through the study years, as a micro-learning and opportunity platform

## Decision

After enrolment, the ranked route becomes the road the student lives, and the portal becomes their **companion** through applying, waiting, the study years, graduating and settling. It offers:

- **Readiness for each remaining gate** (post-study visa, skills assessment, PR), built from real progress (English score, relevant work, certifications); useful actions visibly advance it.
- **Micro-learning tracks** matched to the route: two-to-five-minute lessons, a one-question check, occasional returning questions on things that matter; the student sets the pace; no streaks.
- **Obligations**: reminders that protect the visa and the student (keeping enrolment, work-hour limits, health cover renewal, tax time).
- **Matched opportunities** through the study years: short courses and certifications that strengthen the route, internships, part-time work and jobs in their field (extends ADR-039).
- **Personal growth**: the existing self-help, psychology and book content, timed to the phase (homesickness early, interview nerves late).

## Reasoning

- The decision takes months; the journey takes five years, and today nothing accompanies the student through it (Nikhil: "we are the companion").
- A five-year relationship means Expert is the trusted choice when migration services are needed at the post-study and PR stages; that is likely worth more than the admission.
- Students' real outcomes (job, visa, PR) are the outcome data the strategy names as the moat; a companion collects them naturally, with consent.
- The content engine and ideas queue already exist; the learning layer is mostly organising content into tracks.

## Amends earlier strategy

The vision deck sequenced the wider ecosystem (counselling, finances, jobs) after the corridor is won. Reconciled: the **content-led companion** (tracks, matched opportunities, obligations) may start early because it reuses the content engine; **paid ecosystem services** (job placement, accommodation, insurance) stay sequenced later.

## Constrains

Outcome tracking needs explicit consent (separate from the terms-of-use insights consent in ADR-021). Visa and PR readiness is information with sources, not migration advice; advice comes from a registered agent (ADR-046). Requirements in System Requirements F4.

## Status (30 Sep)

**Future enhancement. Nothing is implemented.** The prototype only shows what it could look like, using **mocked triggers**: sample feed items and notifications fired by a demo control (e.g. "semester results in", "internship match", "health cover renews in 30 days", "new lesson in your track"). No readiness engine, tracks, obligations logic or outcome tracking is built. Paid ecosystem services stay sequenced later.
