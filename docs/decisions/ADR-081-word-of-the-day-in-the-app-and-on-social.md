# ADR-081 · Word of the day, in the app and on social

## Decision

- A daily **word of the day** card in the feed: one term a student will meet on their road or in their new life (e.g. "CoE", "GTE", "bond" for a rental, "superannuation", "bulk billing", "Co-op"), with a one-line definition, a one-line "why it matters to you" tied to their route where it applies, and its source.
- The same card is published to the social pages in each page's brand (ADR-055 derivation), and later in each market's variant (ADR-063).
- Words come from the answer library and the questions students actually ask (ADR-077), so the series teaches what people really get stuck on; a reviewed glossary grows from it.

## Reasoning

Cheap, daily, useful, and on brand: a small habit that teaches the vocabulary of a new life, feeds the glossary, and gives the social pages a reliable rhythm (Nikhil, 2 Oct). Echoes the "word of the day" device from the team rally talk.

## Constrains

A \`word\_of\_the\_day\` content format (template-only, SVG; no image model). Hackathon: three static cards in the feed and one social derivative.
