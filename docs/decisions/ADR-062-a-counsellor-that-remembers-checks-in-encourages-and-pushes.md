# ADR-062 · A counsellor that remembers, checks in, encourages, and pushes when needed

## Decision

The student record is the memory; the watcher is the check-in engine (ADR-058); the companion is the relationship (ADR-050). Content adds **tone**: encouragement when a step is done, a firmer nudge when a deadline that matters to the student's own goals is slipping ("The 30 Nov IELTS date decides the UK option. You haven't booked. This is the week.").

## Rules

- Firmness is only about the student's stated goals and real deadlines, never about buying anything.
- Always paired with the concrete next step.
- Eases off when the student says so; one switch quiets it (ADR-044).

## Reasoning

Retention needs a counsellor's presence over years, which only works if it remembers everything and knows when to encourage and when to push (Nikhil, 1 Oct). Future: needs the user base and the companion.
