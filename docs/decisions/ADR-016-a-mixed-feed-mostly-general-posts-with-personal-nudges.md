# ADR-016 · A mixed feed: mostly general posts, with personal nudges placed by stage and preferences and always labelled

## Decision

The student feed is one stream, mostly general posts (self-help, books, culture, research, psychology, logic), with personal nudges (a trade-off card inviting the student back into the Studio) placed by the student's stage and preferences and always labelled as personal.

## Reasoning

- A single stream reads naturally; separate tabs split attention.
- Labelling nudges keeps trust: the student knows which items are about them.

## Constrains

Nudges deep-link into the Studio with answers intact (ADR-004). Resolves D9.
