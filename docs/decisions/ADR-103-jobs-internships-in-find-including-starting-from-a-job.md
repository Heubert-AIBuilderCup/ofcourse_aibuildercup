# ADR-103 · Jobs & internships in Find, including starting from a job

## Decision

Find gains a **Jobs & internships** view with three parts: **Roles** (jobs a course or field leads to: demand band and salary band with source and data state, a day in the life, the courses that lead there, and a way to try the role); **During study** (internships, placements and part-time work matched to her route and field, staff-added via updates, ADR-039, with work-hour rules shown as information, not advice); and **Start from a job** (choosing a role lights up the routes that lead there, the goal-first navigation of ADR-049). Every job card has the course-card actions where they apply (keep an eye on it, try the role, see courses that lead here).

## Reasoning

Jobs and internships were missing from the design apart from "Life after" and a mocked feed item (Nikhil, 3 Oct); career-focused students think from the job backwards.

## Constrains

**Not in the hackathon** (design and requirements are locked). Future build: occupation and job data in the catalog (W1), the audience matcher for internships, sourced bands (ADR-079). Execution detail in `FUTURE-additions.md`.
