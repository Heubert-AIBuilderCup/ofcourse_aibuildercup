# ADR-107 · Student opening scope: no UK swap, no seeded Priya yet

## Decision

- **"Open to others" as a destination keeps the futures Australian**, with one line saying only Australia is covered in this preview. The UK route of DS-F14 is dropped.
- **Priya's seeded account and the "From what you told us · confirm" pre-fill are deferred** to a later slice. Every tester signs up as themselves.

## Reasoning

The catalogue is Australia only (ADR-097). The user test needs every student who signs up to reach their own futures, not one scripted persona (Jenish, 5 Oct).

## Supersedes / amends

Amends ADR-093 (Priya's seeded profile as a pre-fill source moves to a later slice).
