# ADR-021 · Insights: consent via terms of use; no minimum group size internally

## Decision

Using student preferences for anonymised, aggregated marketing insights is covered in the terms of use; everyone is included. Insights shown inside the company have no minimum group size. If insights are ever shared outside the company, a minimum group size applies.

## Reasoning

- Staff can already see individual profiles (ADR-013), so hiding them in aggregates adds nothing internally.
- Caveat recorded: anything targeting an individual (personalised marketing, a student's photo or name in content) needs explicit consent (ADR-019). UK/EU rules are stricter than Nepal's; get a legal check for destination markets.

## Constrains

Resolves D15, D16.
