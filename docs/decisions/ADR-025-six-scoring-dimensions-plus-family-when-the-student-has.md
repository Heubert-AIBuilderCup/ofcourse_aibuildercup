# ADR-025 · Six scoring dimensions, plus Family when the student has dependants

## Decision

Courses are scored, sliders move, and trade-off cards are organised on six dimensions: Cost, Time to outcome, Work rights & experience, PR pathway, Location, Career prospects. A seventh, Family, appears only when the student has dependants. Scholarship availability folds into Cost; institution reputation and salary data into Career prospects; safety and climate into Location.

## Reasoning

- One list used everywhere removes the mismatch between the v4 course scores (PR, career, cost, speed, experience) and the OfCourse sliders (cost, speed, work rights, PR, location).
- The Edge catalog has a whole Family & dependants domain; it matters enormously to some students and not at all to others, so it is conditional.

## Constrains

Lenses (question sets) and dimensions (scores) are distinct: answers set weights on dimensions. Supersedes the five-lens-with-weights wording in System Requirements S2.1.
