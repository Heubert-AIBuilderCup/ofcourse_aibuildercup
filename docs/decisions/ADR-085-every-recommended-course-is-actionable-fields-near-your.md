# ADR-085 · Every recommended course is actionable; "Fields near your interests" puts the centre on top

## Decision

- Every course card (Find hand, stacks, compendium, fields view, board tile) carries actions: **Put on my board** · **Compare** · **Keep an eye on it** · **What it asks for** (entry requirements against her profile: met / not yet / unknown) · **Try a taster** (if the field has one) · **Ask about this course** (opens chat scoped to it) · **Talk to a counsellor**.
- In "Fields near your interests", the student's centre field sits **at the top**; related fields follow in order of closeness; niche fields are grouped at the end, each with its definition.

## Reasoning

A recommendation that can't be acted on is a dead end; and the information hierarchy must start from where the student is (Nikhil, 2 Oct).
