# ADR-018 · Tiered review before publishing

## Decision

Any generated content stating a visa, PR, cost or eligibility fact, and any research or psychology claim, needs a reviewer before publishing. Lifestyle and self-help posts publish on the editor's approval.

## Reasoning

- Blast-radius tiering, the same principle as the Proposal review in the CRM.
- Teams doing due diligence are the HITL model (Nikhil); the tier decides who must look.

## Constrains

Structure approval (ADR-007) and publish approval are separate steps. Resolves D11.
