# ADR-127 · Dates: user test 12 Oct, roster lock 11 Oct, submission 18 Oct, eligibility checked for every member

## Decision

The user test with three students, a counsellor and a marketer moves from 8–9 Oct to **12 Oct 2026**, so the student feed is in it. The prototype submission deadline is **18 Oct 2026, 23:59 IST**; the team roster locks on **11 Oct**; evaluation runs 19 Oct to 6 Nov, finalists are named 7 Nov, the finale is 4 Dec in Singapore. Before the roster lock, every member's eligibility is confirmed against the rules: no full- or part-time students (the whole team is disqualified if one is found), 21 or older, resident in JAPAC, teams of two to four.

## Reasoning

- The dates were verified on aibuildercup.com and in the T&C on 7 Oct 2026 (`research_notes/Reusable implementations for our stack/VERIFY_hackathon_rules.md`); the team's earlier "about 14 Oct" was a guess.
- A test on 8–9 Oct would have shown the admin without the feed; three days buy the student-facing half of the story.

## Rejected alternative

Keeping the 8–9 Oct test and showing the feed only in the video.

## Constrains

Amends ADR-098 where it names the test dates. The admin intent's deadline constraint is updated. The fresh-code clause ("code developed prior to the official launch will be disqualified") applies to the starter the repo began from; Nikhil decides by 9 Oct whether to ask the organiser in writing.
