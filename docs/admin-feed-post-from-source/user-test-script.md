# User test script, Sunday 12 October 2026

_For Nikhil, running the session. One page per role. Give each person their tasks, not instructions: do not show them where things are; watch where they look. Live site: `https://ofcourse-44805428675.asia-southeast1.run.app` (to be confirmed after the deploy)._

## Before they arrive

- Signed in on the live site as superadmin, with four to six posts published (different destinations and themes, one India and one Nepal version, one made from a link) and one page style saved.
- The marketer's account given Editor on the Users page; your own account is the reviewer.
- A fresh news or research article link about studying abroad, ready to hand the marketer.
- This sheet and the notes template at the end, one copy per person.

Say at the start: "We're testing the product, not you. Think aloud. If you get stuck, tell me what you expected to see."

## The marketer (about 20 minutes)

Tasks, read aloud one at a time:
1. "Here is an article. Turn it into an Instagram-style carousel for students, for the Self-help page."
2. "Pick the version you would actually post, and change any wording you don't like."
3. "Make it feel more hopeful."
4. "Give it a cover you like."
5. "Set it up so students going to Australia see it, then send it for review."
6. (After you publish it) "Make the Instagram version of this post and get the files you'd upload."

Watch for:
- Do they look for a place to type instructions or a prompt? Where?
- Do they understand the three versions and their angles?
- Do they find editing on the slide, and "Another take"?
- Do they notice a flagged sentence and the "Use the source's wording" fix?
- How long from pasting the link to sending for review?

Ask afterwards: "What would you have done differently in your current tool? What was missing? What would you trust, and what would you double-check?"

## You, as the reviewer (during the marketer's session)

1. Open the post in review. Check the facts and the source check, then "Approve and publish".
2. Note anything you had to look up elsewhere to feel safe approving.

## Each student (about 10 minutes each)

Tasks:
1. "Sign in." (Continue with Google)
2. "Find posts for students going to Australia."
3. "Find the post about paid work, then show me the version with prices in your home currency."
4. "Find something related to the post you're reading."
5. "You saw this post on Instagram yesterday. Find it again." (Give them the post's title only; let them try Google, then the site.)
6. "Follow OfCourse on Instagram from here."

Watch for:
- Do the chips make sense without explanation? Do they use search or chips first?
- Do they notice the "For you" and "Featured" tags, and the "why" line?
- Do they read the slides or scroll past them?
- On the search task, do they understand the last-slide line ("search 'OfCourse <title>'")?

Ask afterwards: "Would you come back to this? What would make you trust it? What did you expect that wasn't there?"

## The counsellor (about 10 minutes)

Tasks:
1. "Sign in and read what students are being shown this week."
2. "Find a post you'd send to a student who is worried about money."
3. "Tell me one thing you'd change before you'd recommend it to a student."

Watch for: whether they trust the facts (do they open the source line?), and what they say is missing for a counsellor.
